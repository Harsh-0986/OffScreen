# Brag Plan: Offscreen

## What is this app?

An AI-powered outdoor discovery journal: you get one small challenge a day ("Find
something that looks like a face"), you close the app and go find it, photograph
it, and Gemma looks at your photo, tells you what it sees, scores it, and keeps
the result in a journal.

## The angle

An anti-social app that tells you to close it. The product's own manifesto is the
joke — *"Outside, Not Online"* — and the funniest thing in the whole interface is a
screen whose entire purpose is to get you off it: one sentence, one button, a
running timer counting your time outside.

The video delivers this with total sincerity. No wink, no "haha, clever." The
absurdity is that a phone app is arguing against your phone.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

Paper-white background. The wordmark **OFFSCREEN** sets in heavy grotesque, then
one line under it, held long enough to read:

> The goal was never to keep you in the app.

No motion. That restraint *is* the hook — it reads as an odd thing for a product
ad to say, which is exactly the reaction we want in the first two seconds.

## Key moments (the middle)

- **Today's discovery** — the real challenge card, typeset large: *"Find something
  that looks like a face."* The meta line (about ~20 min · difficulty 2/4 ·
  looking for: nature) arrives one item at a time, deadpan.
- **Mission mode** — the centerpiece gag. Hard cut to giant type: *"Close this app.
  Go outside."* with a small elapsed timer reading 00:00 and the single bordered
  button "I'M BACK". Nothing else on screen. This is the money shot.
- **Result** — a photograph framed like a physical print, the score counting up to
  **9/10** in moss green, and the AI's actual feedback line: *"You found it. The
  branches really do resemble a face."*

## Outro / punchline

The result card settles into a strip of three journal thumbnails, then the final
line holds on paper:

> Come back tomorrow. Until then, go outside.

Then the wordmark returns, smaller and quiet. No call to action, no "download
now" — the app's whole thesis is that you shouldn't be here.

## User flow worth showing

1. **Entry** — Today's discovery, the challenge card with its real prompt and meta.
2. **Key action** — Mission mode: close the app, go outside, come back. The timer
   ticks up. This is the product's unique beat and it has no equivalent anywhere else.
3. **Result** — photograph, score count-up, AI feedback, then the journal strip.

## Tone

- Preset: **deadpan**
- Creative direction: an anti-social network that wants you to leave, presented
  with complete sincerity
- Interpretation: long holds, huge empty space, almost no motion. The comedy comes
  from stillness — every fast, flashy convention is the thing being rejected.
  Restraint everywhere: no bouncing, no gradients, no whooshes.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: `#faf8f4` (warm paper)
- Text: `#16150f` (near-black ink)
- Accent: `#486b3f` (moss) — used only for the score
- Supporting: `#e4dfd4` (line), `#8b877b` (ink faint), `#f6ece5` (clay, error only)
- Display font: **Inter Tight** (heavy grotesque, for statements)
- Body font: **Fraunces** (quiet serif, for reflections — italic for AI feedback)
- Strongest visual element: the mission screen. Giant display type, one button,
  enormous negative space. Recreate it faithfully — the emptiness is the design.

## Share copy (draft)

I built an app whose success metric is you closing it.

## Audio direction

- Role: warm bed, sparse. Confident enough to be quiet.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, starting near the
  top so the bed sits under everything without a build-in.
- Music treatment: low volume under speech-free video, short fade-in on scene 1,
  fade out over the final line. Never swell on a joke — the joke must stay dry.
- Music cue guidance: preset exists, ~120 BPM, planning window 0–25s. Target the
  strong cues at **17.02s** (score count-up lands) and **20.02s** (final line
  settles). Beat grid runs every ~0.5s; do **not** snap sequential text to
  individual beats — reveal text quickly then hold the full set.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none. This tone must not pulse.
- SFX posture: sparse. One soft interface tick as the challenge meta line assembles,
  one muted press on the "I'M BACK" button, one quiet tick per score count-up step.
  Nothing on scene 1 — the hook is silence.
- Audio-coupled moments: score count-up (ticks), mission button press, meta-line assembly.
- Restraint rule: no risers, no impacts, no whooshes, no beat hits on the punchline.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 4.5s

Paper background, nothing else. **OFFSCREEN** in Inter Tight 800, near-black,
centred-left. After a beat, the manifesto line types in below it in Fraunces
italic, ink-faint: *"The goal was never to keep you in the app."* Both hold, still,
with the tiniest drift. Hold the manifesto line a full 2.5s settled.

Sequential/interaction: yes — wordmark sets, then the line types on. Nothing else moves.
Audio intent: silence into a very low bed. Establish quiet before anything happens.
Audio-coupled idea: none. Deliberately dry.
Music: sparse warm bed, fading in over 1.5s.
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Today's discovery — 4.5s

The real challenge card. Eyebrow `TODAY'S DISCOVERY` in letterspaced caps, then the
prompt in large Fraunces: *"Find something that looks like a face."* Below it, the
meta line assembles one item at a time — `~20 MIN` · `DIFFICULTY 2 / 4` ·
`LOOKING FOR: NATURE` — each landing on a soft tick and then holding while the rest
arrive, until all three sit on screen together for the last second.

Sequential/interaction: yes — three meta items arrive one at a time, then hold as a set.
Audio intent: quiet competence. Interface ticks, nothing musical.
Audio-coupled idea: soft tick per meta item, deliberately not on consecutive beats.
Music: bed continues, unchanged.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Mission mode — 4.0s

The centerpiece. Hard cut to the mission screen: eyebrow `MISSION STARTED`, then
giant display type on two lines — **"Close this app."** / **"Go outside."** — with
enormous space around it. Below, a small tabular timer increments from `00:00` to
`00:04`, and one bordered pill button reads **I'M BACK**. A cursor drifts to the
button and presses it once, near the end.

Sequential/interaction: yes — cursor moves and clicks the single button. One click only.
Audio intent: the setup for the joke. Stillness, then one soft press.
Audio-coupled idea: muted button press; the timer ticks are visual only, no per-second sound.
Music: bed continues, unchanged. No swell.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Result → journal → final line — 8.0s

A photograph framed like a print (subtle drop shadow, square). Beside it, the score
counts up in moss green to **9** with a faint `/10`, then the AI's feedback line
arrives in Fraunces italic: *"You found it. The branches really do resemble a face."*
Then `+9 DISCOVERY POINTS` in letterspaced caps. The whole card settles down into a
row of three journal thumbnails. Finally, on paper, the closing line holds:

> Come back tomorrow. Until then, go outside.

Sequential/interaction: yes — score count-up, feedback line, points line, then the
card collapsing into the journal strip, then the final line.
Audio intent: the only warmth in the video. Quiet ticks through the count-up, then
everything settles and the music fades under the last line.
Audio-coupled idea: ticks on the count-up targeting the 17.02s strong cue; the final
line lands on 20.02s. Hold the final line a full 2.5s — do not cut early.
Music: bed fades out over the final line.
Transition mood: n/a — this is the end.

**Music mood for this video:** sparse, warm, unhurried.
**Audio summary:** a quiet bed and four small interface sounds across 21 seconds,
with total silence in the first beat so the joke stays dry.
